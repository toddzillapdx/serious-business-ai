⚠ Rewardful Validation Required

Rewardful is confirmed live on the site (Stripe-native). Before committing the Rewardful → Zapier → Airtable flow to the client, verify that Rewardful has a native Zapier app and that the specific trigger events needed (new conversion, new referral) are available. If not, fallback is Rewardful webhook → Zapier Catch Hook → Airtable — still doable, slightly more fragile.

⚠ Circle Zapier Trigger Validation Required

The Event Attendance flow depends on Circle exposing event registration as a Zapier trigger. Confirm Circle's Zapier trigger list against the specific events being captured (new member join, event registration, event attendance). If event attendance isn't a native trigger, fallback is Circle webhooks → Zapier Catch Hook → Airtable.

03 Alignment

Questions for Gina

  1. What's the client's primary US goal — and on what timeline? Digital membership growth, physical academy, or club partnerships first? Entry sequence changes significantly depending on the answer.
  2. What's the current state of brand positioning for a US audience? Existing US-facing messaging, or built from scratch? Directly in Gina's lane — shapes the martech content strategy.
  3. How strong is the USA Padel relationship? Warm intro or formal partnership conversation? Determines how aggressively we scope the distribution strategy around it.
  4. What's the client's budget signal for US setup? Advisory only, or are they expecting a build estimate? Shapes whether we present options or a single recommended path.
  5. Who owns community management on their side? US timezone moderation is a real gap. Is there a US-based team member, or is this a hire/agency gap we flag?
  6. Are they open to AI augmentation? Coaching video analysis and email automation specifically. Some coaching brands are sensitive about AI touching the coaching product.
  7. What's Gina's read on the founders? Two brothers running a bootstrapped operation — operators who want a growth partner, or visionaries who need strategy translated into a buildable plan?
